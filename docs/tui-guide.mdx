## Turn Structure & Cards

Each interaction with SAGO is encapsulated inside structured turn widgets:

### 1. Enhanced Prompt Card (`✨ Enhanced Prompt`)
Before the main LLM call executes, the lightweight non-agent context summarizer produces a **2–7 line working summary** detailing active goals, touched files, and candidate symbols. Click the header or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to toggle between collapsed and expanded views.

### 2. Systematic Thinking Header (`● {agent} — Technical Reasoning`)
Reasoning blocks from thinking models (Claude, DeepSeek-R1, Gemini) stream directly into dedicated collapsible reasoning bubbles. You can inspect internal reasoning steps in real time without cluttering your final answers.

### 3. Tool Execution Cards (`⚡ Tools`)
Every tool invocation renders its status in real time:
- **Running:** Active spinner with execution parameters.
- **Success:** Green badge with output preview and modification counts.
- **Failure:** Red badge with actionable error diagnostics and recovery steps.

### 4. Smart Auto-Collapsing & Scrolling
To keep long sessions responsive and prevent terminal lag:
- Completed intermediate exchange cards collapse smoothly.
- The latest active turn remains pinned and auto-scrolls.
- The vertical scrollbar is permanently visible with full mouse and touch scroll support.
